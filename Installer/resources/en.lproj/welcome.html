Mac Display Volume

Control an external display's audio volume on macOS like a normal speaker.

This installer installs:

  • Menu bar app: /Applications/Mac Display Volume.app
  • Audio driver: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL/MacDisplayVolumeAudio.driver

System audio briefly stops while CoreAudio restarts. The app opens when installation finishes.
